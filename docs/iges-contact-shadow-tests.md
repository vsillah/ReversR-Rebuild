# Opt-in contact cue

renderQuality.contactShadow uses height-contact-v1: orthogonal source-plane projection, quadratic finite height falloff, maximum overlap, then one finite box blur per plane tangent. It is a light-independent presentation approximation, not ray-traced AO. No default enables it; directional shadow is mutually exclusive.

Required settings: unit normal, offset with dot(normal, point)=offset, sourceUnits and meshUnits matching admitted conversion metadata, declared-presentation-plane provenance (orientation and minimum-visible-geometry offsetPolicy), positive geometryScale, heightCutoff and softness in mesh units, opacity from zero through 0.4, and maxRadiusPixels exactly 12. No automatic normal correction, unit conversion or silent softness clamp. The caller must resolve and record any presentation-scale cap before invoking the renderer. Invalid/intersecting planes fail; supported empty/degenerate/edge-on results have explicit statuses.

The tangent seed is the coordinate axis least aligned with the normal, ties by index. u=normalize(cross(normal,seed)); v=cross(normal,u). Grid spacing is min(1/(sum of projected tangent magnitudes),softness/3); blur radius is floor(softness/spacing + 1e-9), with a numerical boundary allowance. Effective softness never exceeds the declaration. Linear reconstruction adds one neighboring cell on each side; the full reconstructed radius is checked against the 12-pixel cap and all support corners are checked against the viewport. The evidence retains a conservative two-cell sampling allowance. Grid limits are 4096 per side and four million cells. Intersection tolerance is measured visible-geometry extent*1e-8; caller geometryScale cannot enlarge it. The helper reports support clipping and the renderer rejects it before compositing. Contact projection is unrounded; ordinary geometry retains its prior integer rounding. Foreground ownership and accepted edge coverage protect existing pixels.
